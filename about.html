About Taskit

We think the next generation of task tools won't just hold your to-do list. Agents will work through it. Taskit is our demo of what that could feel like.

Why we built it

Most task managers stop at storing tasks. We wanted to show the next step: you describe the work, hand it to a specialist agent, and watch it move from To do to Done.

Taskit is a demo. The agents are simulated so you can explore the experience safely. Your tasks stay in your own browser and are never sent anywhere.

Features

  • Add, edit, complete and delete tasks with due dates and priorities
  • Assign each task to one of four specialist AI agents
  • Simulated agent runs, with every step logged
  • Live search, filters and sorting
  • Charts and a drag-and-drop Kanban board
  • Light and dark mode on any screen size
